# AI Topic Teaser Prompt

Create a 1200x627 blog teaser image with a modern, minimal, editorial tech
style. It should feel like a clean developer-blog feature image for a single
topic, not stocky, not overly realistic, and not generic AI art.

BACKGROUND:

- Dark matte charcoal or near-black background
- Very subtle thin-line grid or system pattern at low opacity
- Clean, high-contrast composition

LAYOUT:

- Centered headline with a smaller subtitle below
- A single main subject or document motif, placed in a lower corner and
  cropped slightly off-canvas
- Keep the left or right side open for the title and sparkle system
- No comparison bars, no left/right balancing, no versus layout

SUBJECT:

- Use a simple, semi-flat icon or object representing: {SUBJECT}
- Style: minimal, geometric, slightly soft shadows, not photorealistic
- The subject should read clearly at small sizes

SPARKLE SYSTEM:

- Large geometric sparkle or star shape anchored to the opposite edge
- Cropped so only part of it is visible, bleeding off canvas
- Occupies about 60% of the height
- Warm golden or yellow glow
- Add 2 to 4 smaller sparkles trailing inward
- Sparkles are decorative, not part of the subject

TYPOGRAPHY:

- Large bold title centered or slightly upper-center:
  {TITLE}
- Smaller subtitle below:
  {SUBTITLE}
- Clean sans-serif style with modern editorial balance
- Subtitle should be slightly lower opacity than the title

STYLE:

- Flat to semi-flat design
- Minimal color palette:
    - dark background
    - white or gray text
    - single accent color in gold or yellow for sparkles
- Avoid:
    - gradient overload
    - glossy 3D renders
    - stock infographic elements
    - robots, brains, chips, or cliché AI visuals

MOOD:

- Thoughtful, sharp, technical, opinionated
- Feels like a high-quality developer blog, not marketing material

OUTPUT:

- Clean spacing
- Strong readability at small sizes
- Visually balanced composition
